About PriceRadar

A smart grocery price comparison tool built for the Indian quick-commerce ecosystem.

🎯 What We Do

PriceRadar scrapes real-time product listings from Blinkit, BigBasket, Zepto, and Swiggy Instamart in parallel and presents the results in a clean, comparable format.

🤖 AI Assistant

We use the Groq API with Llama 3.1 to analyze your search results and give you a plain-English buying recommendation — no jargon, just good advice.

⚠️ Disclaimer

PriceRadar is an academic/educational project. Scraping platform websites may violate their Terms of Service. Always verify prices directly on the respective apps before purchasing. Prices shown may be delayed or inaccurate.

🛠️ Tech Stack

  • 🐍 Python + Flask
  • 🎭 Playwright (stealth)
  • 🤖 Groq / Llama 3.1
  • 🎨 Vanilla CSS
  • ⚡ ThreadPoolExecutor
  • 🔒 In-memory caching
